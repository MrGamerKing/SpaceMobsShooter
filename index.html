ZOMBIE

Slow and tanky. Takes a few hits.

SKELETON

Stops, strafes and fires aimed arrows. It glows before every shot.

CREEPER

Hunts you and explodes. Drops TNT — shoot it to blow up nearby mobs.

VEX

Fast swarms that shake, then dive straight at you.

EVOKER

Summons Vex and casts magic orbs. Kill it first!

WARDENBOSS

Every 5th wave. Three phases — dodge the red zone before its Sonic Boom.

WEAPON STAR

Upgrades your gun up to level 5 with homing missiles.

OVERDRIVE

Double fire rate, bigger piercing shots, extra speed.

SHIELD

Blocks everything for 10s. Ram mobs to crush them.

MAGNET

Pulls every pickup on screen straight to you.

SCORE POTION

Doubles all points for 12 seconds.

EMERALD ORB

+250 points instantly.

HEART

Repairs 1 hull. At full health it raises your max hull (up to 8).

  • COMBO — chain kills quickly to raise your multiplier up to x8. Getting hit or letting a mob escape resets it.
  • GRAZE — skim bullets without touching them for bonus points and Nova charge.
  • NOVA — a screen-wide shockwave that wipes bullets and hurts every mob. Charges from kills and grazes.
  • PERFECT — clear a wave without taking damage for double bonus and a free power-up.
