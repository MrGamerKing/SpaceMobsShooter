ZOMBIE

Slow and tanky. Takes a few hits.

SKELETON

Stops, strafes and fires aimed arrows. It glows before every shot.

CREEPER

Hunts you and explodes. Drops TNT — shoot it to blow up nearby mobs.

VEX

Fast swarms that shake, then dive straight at you.

SLIMENEW

Bounces toward you and splits into smaller slimes when hit hard enough.

EVOKER

Summons Vex and casts magic orbs. Kill it first!

BLAZENEW

Its rods spin faster as it heats up, then it fires a burst of fireballs.

ENDERMANNEW

Teleports next to you, stares, then lunges. Sometimes dodges your shots.

SHULKERNEW

Armored while its shell is shut. Fires homing bullets you can shoot down.

GHASTNEW

Big fireballs that burst into shards — shoot them to blast them back!

★
ELITE MOBS

From wave 7, some mobs glow gold: 3x health, faster attacks, triple points and a guaranteed power-up.

A boss arrives every 5 waves — 10 different bosses. Beat all ten and they return as MK II, MK III... with a new colour, more bullets, faster attacks and new moves. Every boss you beat makes your weapon stronger.

WEAPON STAR

Upgrades your gun to level 5 (homing missiles). After that each star adds +10% power.

OVERDRIVE

Double fire rate, bigger piercing shots, extra speed.

SHIELD

Blocks everything for 10s. Ram mobs to crush them.

MAGNET

Pulls every pickup on screen straight to you.

TIME WARPNEW

Slows every mob and bullet to 40% for 7 seconds. You stay full speed.

THUNDERSTORMNEW

Lightning strikes up to 3 mobs every half second.

ALLAY DRONESNEW

Two helpers orbit you and fire homing bolts.

TOTEM OF UNDYINGNEW

An extra life: when you would die you come back with 3 hearts and a free Nova.

SCORE POTION

Doubles all points for 12 seconds.

EMERALD ORB

+250 points instantly.

HEART

+1 heart and cures Mining Fatigue. At full health it adds a heart slot (up to 10).

  • COMBO — chain kills quickly to raise your multiplier up to x8. Getting hit or letting a mob escape resets it.
  • NOVA — a screen-wide shockwave. It only recharges from mobs YOU kill and from grazing bullets — kills made by the Nova itself don't count.
  • GRAZE — skim bullets without touching them for bonus points and Nova charge.
  • PERFECT — clear a wave without taking damage for double bonus and a free power-up.
